R&D and licensed technology

Technologies

Alongside its own manufacturing, Unicare has taken technology licenses and transfers from national research institutions to bring specialised contaminant-removal capability to market.

CMERI technology license

Iron Removal Filter

High flow-rate filter for Fe(II)/Fe(III) contamination.

  • No electricity consumption, no waste generated
  • Adsorbent capacity factor: 15 mg/g
  • Regenerated via back-wash facility
  • Handles feed iron concentration up to ~12 ppm
  • Flow rate: 3,000–3,500 L/hour, 3-stage purification
CMERI technology license

Arsenic Removal Filter

High flow-rate filter for As(III)/As(V) contamination.

  • Affordable, high-flow-rate design
  • Filters water up to ~300 ppb arsenic concentration
  • Long-lasting, non-toxic, low-cost adsorbent media
  • Media regenerated via back-wash
BARC technology transfer

Arsenic Removal — Physicochemical Process

Ultrafiltration-based decontamination process for arsenic in drinking water.

  • Addresses arsenic contamination across the Ganga-Meghna-Brahmaputra basin states
  • Targets WHO provisional guideline of 10 ppb arsenic in drinking water
  • Suitable for both domestic and community-scale deployment
CMERI technology license

Fluoride & Iron Removal Filter

Integrated adsorbent-based system for fluoride and iron affected water.

  • Deployed in fluoride/iron-affected regions such as UP, Bihar, Assam and Jharkhand
  • Adsorbent-based, regenerable media
  • Removes both contaminants in a single pass
Self-developed, CSIR-CMERI tested

Unicare 700

A rapid-deployment water treatment unit built for disaster-response situations.

  • Tested by CSIR-Central Mechanical Engineering Research Institute
  • Designed for rapid deployment in emergency and disaster scenarios
