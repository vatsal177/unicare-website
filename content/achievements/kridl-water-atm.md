Unicare built a community-based water ATM network in collaboration with the Karnataka Rural Infrastructure Development Limited (KRIDL) to supply clean, low-cost drinking water at public places. Each system dispenses up to 15,000 litres per day. Across the state, more than 900 community water plants are now installed, together generating over 75 million litres of purified water daily and reaching more than 6 million people.
