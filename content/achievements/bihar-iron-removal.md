Also in partnership with Bihar's Public Health Engineering Department, Unicare has installed more than 250 community-based Iron Removal Plants (IRP) to address elevated iron content in groundwater. The plants use biological filtration to remove iron precipitate and now produce over 40 million litres of purified water daily, benefiting more than 4.5 million people in the surrounding areas.
