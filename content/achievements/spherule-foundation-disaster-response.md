One of Unicare's most meaningful deployments: 4 units of the self-developed Unicare 700 disaster-response water treatment system were supplied to the Spherule Foundation during the 2020 Chiplun, Maharashtra flood. The rapid-deployment units gave the surrounding community access to clean, safe drinking water during the emergency and its aftermath.
