Under a PPP-mode agreement with the Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation, Unicare installed 100 water ATM units across bus stations statewide. The network now produces at least 6 million litres of fresh drinking water and reaches more than 6 million people passing through UP's bus stations daily.
