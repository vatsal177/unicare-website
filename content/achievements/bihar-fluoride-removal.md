Working with the Public Health Engineering Department in Bihar, Unicare has installed more than 20 fluoride removal plants (FRP) across areas with elevated fluoride content in groundwater — a known cause of dental and skeletal fluorosis. The community-based plants use fibre-glass reinforced polyester construction and now produce over 3.2 million litres of purified water daily, reaching more than 36,000 people.
