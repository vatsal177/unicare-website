Under Sandvik Coromant's CSR initiative in association with the Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation, Unicare installed 3 sewage treatment plants of varying capacity in Pune, Maharashtra. Wastewater drawn from a local nala is treated and reused for garden irrigation, cutting fresh water consumption for landscaping.
