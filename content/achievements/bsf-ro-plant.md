Unicare has supplied 250 LPH reverse osmosis plants installed aboard Border Security Force ships, with ongoing servicing wherever required. The RO systems purify contaminated water by forcing it through a membrane, giving personnel stationed at sea reliable access to safe drinking water in demanding conditions.
