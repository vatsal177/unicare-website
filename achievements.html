Achievements

Real projects, real partners, real impact — a selection of Unicare's deployments across India.

KRIDL

Karnataka Rural Infrastructure Development Limited — Water ATM

900+Plants installed
6 Million+People impacted
75 Million+Litres purified / day

Unicare built a community-based water ATM network in collaboration with the Karnataka Rural Infrastructure Development Limited (KRIDL) to supply clean, low-cost drinking water at public places. Each system dispenses up to 15,000 litres per day. Across the state, more than 900 community water plants are now installed, together generating over 75 million litres of purified water daily and reaching more than 6 million people.

PHED Bihar

Public Health Engineering Department, Bihar — Fluoride Removal Plants

20+Plants installed
36,000+People impacted
3.2 Million+Litres purified / day

Working with the Public Health Engineering Department in Bihar, Unicare has installed more than 20 fluoride removal plants (FRP) across areas with elevated fluoride content in groundwater — a known cause of dental and skeletal fluorosis. The community-based plants use fibre-glass reinforced polyester construction and now produce over 3.2 million litres of purified water daily, reaching more than 36,000 people.

PHED Bihar

Public Health Engineering Department, Bihar — Iron Removal Plants

250+Plants installed
4.5 Million+People impacted
40 Million+Litres purified / day

Also in partnership with Bihar's Public Health Engineering Department, Unicare has installed more than 250 community-based Iron Removal Plants (IRP) to address elevated iron content in groundwater. The plants use biological filtration to remove iron precipitate and now produce over 40 million litres of purified water daily, benefiting more than 4.5 million people in the surrounding areas.

Spherule Foundation

Spherule Foundation — Disaster Water Solution (Unicare 700)

4 unitsPlants installed

One of Unicare's most meaningful deployments: 4 units of the self-developed Unicare 700 disaster-response water treatment system were supplied to the Spherule Foundation during the 2020 Chiplun, Maharashtra flood. The rapid-deployment units gave the surrounding community access to clean, safe drinking water during the emergency and its aftermath.

Border Security Force

Border Security Force — Reverse Osmosis Plants

250 LPH unitsPlants installed

Unicare has supplied 250 LPH reverse osmosis plants installed aboard Border Security Force ships, with ongoing servicing wherever required. The RO systems purify contaminated water by forcing it through a membrane, giving personnel stationed at sea reliable access to safe drinking water in demanding conditions.

UPSRTC

Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation — Water ATM

100 unitsPlants installed
6 Million+People impacted
6 Million+Litres purified / day

Under a PPP-mode agreement with the Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation, Unicare installed 100 water ATM units across bus stations statewide. The network now produces at least 6 million litres of fresh drinking water and reaches more than 6 million people passing through UP's bus stations daily.

Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation

PCMC — Sandvik Coromant Sewage Treatment Plant

3 unitsPlants installed

Under Sandvik Coromant's CSR initiative in association with the Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation, Unicare installed 3 sewage treatment plants of varying capacity in Pune, Maharashtra. Wastewater drawn from a local nala is treated and reused for garden irrigation, cutting fresh water consumption for landscaping.
