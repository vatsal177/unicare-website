# How to add photos to the website

You do this the same way you already add blog posts — through `/admin`, no code, no sending files to anyone.

## Adding a photo to a blog post or achievement

Already works today, no setup needed:
1. Go to `yoursite.com/admin`, log in.
2. Open (or create) a Blog Post or Achievement.
3. Click into the Body/Description text box, click the image icon in the small toolbar above it.
4. Upload your photo, or pick one you've already uploaded.
5. It appears in the post automatically once you Publish.

## Adding the homepage hero photo (background image behind the headline)

1. Go to `yoursite.com/admin`, log in.
2. Click **Media** (top nav, next to Contents).
3. Click **Upload**, choose your photo.
4. **Important: name the file exactly `hero.jpg`** before uploading (rename it on your computer first if needed). The website is already set up to look for a photo with this exact name — as soon as it exists, it appears behind the homepage headline automatically. No further steps.
5. If you later want to change it, upload a new photo also named `hero.jpg` — it replaces the old one.

Recommended: a wide, landscape photo of an installed plant or your facility, at least 1600px wide. It gets a dark overlay automatically so your white headline text stays readable over any photo.

## Adding photos anywhere else (product pages, about page, etc.)

This part isn't self-service yet — those pages don't have a pre-wired photo slot the way the hero does. For any of these:

1. Upload the photo via the same **Media** panel (Step 2-3 above) — this saves it to the site.
2. Send me the filename you used and which page/section it's for.
3. I'll wire that specific spot in the code to display it — a quick one-time change per new photo location, not per photo (once a spot like "STP product page header" is wired, uploading a new photo with the same filename replaces it with no further help needed, exactly like the hero works now).

If you know roughly which pages you'll want photos on soon (e.g. all 24 product pages, or just the top 5), tell me and I'll wire those specific slots in the next update so they become self-service too, the same way the hero now is.
