Water Disinfection Unit

Final-stage disinfection systems that eliminate remaining bacteria, viruses and pathogens before water reaches the point of use.

What is a Water Disinfection Unit?

Disinfection is the last treatment stage before distribution; chlorine, ozone or UV light is used to inactivate microorganisms that survive earlier filtration stages.

Benefits

✓Chlorine, ozone or UV disinfection options available
✓Sized to system flow rate
✓Deployed as the final stage in treatment, water ATM and STP/ETP systems
