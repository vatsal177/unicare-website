Package Drinking Water Plant

Complete packaged drinking water production lines, RO treatment, PET blow-moulding, filling and capping, supplied as a single project.

What is a Package Drinking Water Plant?

A packaged drinking water plant treats raw water through pre-treatment, RO filtration, disinfection and activated carbon polishing, then bottles or pouches it for distribution, all supplied as connected equipment sets.

Benefits

✓Full production line: RO, PET blow-moulding, filling, capping, pouch/jar packing
✓Customised per feed water chemical analysis
✓Suited to food processing, pharmaceutical and boiler-feed grade output
✓Complete chemical analysis of feed water carried out before design

Specifications

RO production range100 LPH to 100 m3/hour
Feed TDS range400 - 45,000 TDS
TDS reduction90-99%
PET blow-moulding700 / 2,400 / 4,800 bottles/hour
Filling & cappingup to 3,600 bottles/hour (0.5L-2L)
Jar filling200 x 20L jars/hour
